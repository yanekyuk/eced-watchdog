Faaliyet kapsamında asit-baz nötralizasyonu ve sulu ortam iyonlaşma/denge reaksiyonları içeren kimyasal üretimler yer almakta olup, işlem mevcut 3.483 m2 kapalı alanda ek bina inşaatı yapılmaksızın yürütülecektir. **Summary (EN):** The project involves an industrial capacity increase for various cleaning and personal care products from 18,564 tons/year to 55,442.88 tons/year at an existing facility located in Gaziemir, İzmir. The operations include chemical production processes involving acid-base neutralization and aqueous ionization/equilibrium reactions within an existing 3,483 m2 indoor area without new building construction. The project site is zoned for industrial use and is located 715 meters away from the nearest residential settlement.
