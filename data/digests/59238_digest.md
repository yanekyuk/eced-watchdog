**Summary (EN):** Solar Madencilik Hizmetleri A.Ş. plans to construct an ANFO production facility with an annual capacity of 30,000 tons on parcel 352, block 36 in Yenimahalle/Beşevler Neighborhood, Pazaryolu District, Erzurum Province. The project, planned on a 400 square meter area within a 321,569 square meter parcel, involves mixing approximately 95 percent porous prilled ammonium nitrate (PPAN) and 5 percent diesel fuel to manufacture explosives. An existing explosives storage facility with a capacity of 39 tons is already operating on the same parcel. The project introduction file states that the land use is classified as vacant land and pasture according to the scale environmental plan, with the nearest residence located 210 meters away.
