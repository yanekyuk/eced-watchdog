Proje kapsamında yüzey temizleme ve boyama hatlarının eklenmesi amaçlanmış olup, tesis için Valilik tarafından Ek-2 kapsamında 'ÇED Gerekli Değil / ÇED Olumlu' kararı verilmiştir. **Summary (EN):** KAAF Railway Vehicles Inc. plans to expand operations at an existing facility located on Plot 33, Block 7559 in the Mimarsinan Organized Industrial Zone, Melikgazi, Kayseri, for the manufacturing of railway vehicles, auxiliary equipment, and motor vehicles. The project involves the addition of surface treatment and painting lines, for which the Governorate issued a ÇED Not Required / Positive decision under Annex-2. The facility is designed for 20 personnel, and industrial wastewater will be discharged into the OIZ sewerage system.
