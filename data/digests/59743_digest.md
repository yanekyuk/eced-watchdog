**Summary (EN):** The proposed 2026/3 Permit Numbered (ER:3545889) I-A Group Ağapınar-3 Borrow Pit project by the Highways 4th Regional Directorate aims to extract 1,000,000 tons of fill material annually across a 9.39-hectare pastureland in Odunpazarı, Eskişehir. Key environmental and legal sensitivities include the registered 'Çürüksu Archaeological Find Area' located approximately 240 meters northeast of the project site and 30 meters southeast of the haul road, alongside unresolved pasture conversion requirements. The filing has received a 'CEI Positive (Annex-2)' decision from the local Governorship.
