**Summary (EN):** The proposed Olive Oil and Pomace Production Capacity Increase project by Efedelce Zeytin Zeytinyağı Pirina Gıda Üretim Tic. Ltd. Şti. in Yağdere Neighborhood, Sultanhisar District, Aydın Province, covers parcels 115/4 and 116/1-2 under the Annex-2 review framework. The project involves olive processing, pickled scrap olive processing, and pomace recovery, generating massive quantities of liquid waste and olive pomace. Residential structures and vineyard houses are situated in close proximity to the facility, making environmental management of waste and discharges a critical concern.
