**Summary (EN):** Ekin Beton İnşaat Limited Company plans a capacity increase for a 150 m3/h ready-mixed concrete plant and an additional crushing, washing, and screening facility on a 3.49-hectare area in Yolugüzel Village, Merkez District, Ağrı Province. The 9,000,000 TL project anticipates an annual production of 288,000 m3 of ready-mixed concrete and 390,000 tons of crushed aggregate. The project site is located on agricultural land and presents environmental risks regarding groundwater, dust emissions, and traffic load.
