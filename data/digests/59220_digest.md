**Summary (EN):** Umut Petrokimya ve Insaat Ticaret Limited Sirketi proposes an expansion of a broiler chicken production facility on a 26,229.44 m2 agricultural parcel in Cercioglu Village, Duzici District, Osmaniye Province, with a project cost of 178,445,500 TL [s. 1, 2, 11, 21]. Before starting construction under a prior 19,000-bird/cycle EIA exemption decision, the project proposes scaling up to six two-story coops (21,120 m2 enclosed area) housing 380,160 broilers per cycle across 5-6 cycles annually [s. 11, 12, 14].
