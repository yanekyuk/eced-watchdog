**Summary (EN):** Entegre Harç San. ve Tic. A.Ş. has submitted an EIA Application File (ÇED Başvuru Dosyası) to expand the RN:28048 (ER: 2206221) limestone quarry and crushing-screening-washing facility in Üvezli Mahallesi, Şile District, İstanbul. The proposal increases the EIA area from 37.45 ha to 54.15 ha by adding 16.7 ha (16.3 ha forest land and 0.40 ha agricultural field), while raising annual run-of-mine quarry output from 750,000 to 2,500,000 tonnes, crushing-screening from 600,000 to 1,500,000 tonnes, and washing from 240,000 to 900,000 tonnes. Key environmental stakes include forest land conversion, potential impacts on karstic aquifer units and seasonal dry streambeds, and air, noise, blasting, and cumulative impacts relative to a nearby livestock farm (360 m), water bottling plant (385 m), residences (600 m), and adjacent mining sites to the north.
