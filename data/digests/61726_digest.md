**Summary (EN):** The proposed Washing and Screening Plant project by MRT Maden San. ve Tic. A.Ş. in Çayırbağı Neighborhood, Meram District, Konya Province, is an addition to an existing integrated mining complex across four licensed areas. The project encompasses a total EIA area of 397.67 hectares, of which 226.56 hectares consist of forested lands. While the project aims to wash and size raw and crushed materials, major environmental risks include intensive water consumption, cumulative pressures on forest and agricultural lands, and potential impacts on nearby groundwater resources.
