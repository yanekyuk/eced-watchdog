**Summary (EN):** Elkisan Lime Industry Ltd. Co. plans to operate a limestone quarry, crushing-screening plant capacity increase, and a ready-mixed concrete plant within a 24.92-hectare licensed area in Körpe Village, Central District of Elazığ Province [s. 6]. The project entails increasing annual limestone production from 350,000 tons to 600,000 tons, expanding crushing capacity, and adding a 101 m³/h ready-mixed concrete plant [s. 6]. Blasting-based open-pit mining will be conducted, raising potential environmental pressures on nearby agricultural lands, livestock activities, and water resources [s. 6, 7].
