**Summary (EN):** Bayburt Grup İnşaat Nakliyat Mad. İth. İhr. San. ve Tic. A.Ş. proposes to construct a 150 MWe/150 MWm solar power plant covering 219.71 hectares and a 150 MWh/150 MWe lithium battery energy storage facility covering 1.42 hectares in Dulundas Neighborhood, Çamoluk District, Giresun Province [s. 1, 7, 18]. An earlier EIA process for the same project in Şebinkarahisar was terminated on 11.05.2026 due to negative pastureland (mera) opinions from the Provincial Directorate of Agriculture and Forestry and an overlap with the Burçak HEPP, leading to the relocation to Çamoluk [s. 7]. The revised site still consists predominantly of highland pasture (yaylak/mera) and agricultural parcels designated as Agriculture and Meadow-Pasture in the 1/100,000 Environmental Plan, situated at elevations of 1900-2260 m with slopes up to 40 percent over porous limestone with aquifer characteristics [s. 8, 44, 45, 46, 49]. At this EIA Application File stage, baseline flora-fauna field surveys and detailed rockfall, avalanche, and flood risk assessments have not been completed and are deferred to the full EIA Report [s. 34, 44, 45, 46].
