**Summary (EN):** Seyhmus Yavuz proposes an open-pit marble quarry with an annual production capacity of 400,000 m3 within an 8.79-hectare EIA area (Polygon-1: 8.54 ha; Polygon-2: 0.25 ha) located inside license area 201701049 (99.55 ha) in Camliova Neighborhood, Kas District, Antalya Province [s. 1, 28, 33]. Only 5% of total extraction (20,000 m3/year) is planned as marble blocks, whereas 95% (380,000 m3/year or 1,026,000 tonnes/year) consists of waste rock intended for commercial sale [s. 33]. A previous 24.90-hectare application under the same license was terminated following an adverse Forestry Regional Directorate opinion noting productive Cedar, Turkish Pine, and Juniper stands and adjacency to seed stands [s. 28]. The revised 8.79-hectare project remains entirely within designated 'Forest Area' on the 1/100,000 Environmental Plan, with primary environmental stakes centered on forest ecosystems, nearby seed stands, proximity to local springs (750-940 m to Aklar Spring_4), and high-volume waste extraction [s. 28, 33].
