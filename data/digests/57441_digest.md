**Summary (EN):** Newagri Kimya Tarım plans a capacity increase for its liquid and solid fertilizer mixing, filling, packaging, and storage facility located on plot 8822, parcel 1 (1,210 m2) in Aşağıoba Neighborhood, Döşemealtı District, Antalya, scaling total production from 972 tons/year to 18,240 tons/year. No new buildings will be constructed, but machinery and equipment will be upgraded and expanded. The facility is situated within the 1st-degree Duraliler Protection Area and lies 5,300 meters away from the Kırkgözler Water Springs [s. 1, 6, 38].
