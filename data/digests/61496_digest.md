**Summary (EN):** An Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) Not Required decision has been issued for the Olive Oil Production Plant proposed by Muhsin Bayraktar Tarım Ürünleri Gıda Paz. San. ve Tic. Ltd. Şti. in Çınarköy Neighborhood, Kemalpaşa District, İzmir. The facility's operational capacity is designed as 120 tons/day of olive processing and 24 tons/day of olive oil production. The project area is designated within an urban development zone in the scale 1/100,000 environmental plan, and the operations involve environmental aspects such as solid waste, wastewater, and air emissions.
