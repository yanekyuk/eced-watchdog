**Summary (EN):** MESKİM GIDA İTH. İHR. SAN. VE TİC. LTD. ŞTİ. plans to add the production of various chemicals (calcium sulfate, vinasse, gluconic acid, glucono delta lactone, gluconate derivatives, pharma glycerin, and dextrin glue) to its existing citric acid production facility located in Mersin Province, Akdeniz District, Evci Organized Industrial Zone, parcel 117/2. The project will reach a total annual production capacity of 40,980 tons within a 2,700 m2 enclosed area with no new construction. Although the Governorate issued a 'Environmental Impact Assessment Not Required' decision under Annex-2, the filing presents technical gaps regarding chemical waste management, wastewater discharge permits, and cumulative environmental impacts.
