**Summary (EN):** Hastavuk Gıda Tarım Hayvancılık San. ve Tic. A.Ş. proposes a capacity expansion for its broiler breeder laying facility in the Holoz locality of Kaymaz Neighborhood, Sivrihisar District, Eskişehir Province (parcel 7234/139, 41,572 m2 total land area). While construction is still ongoing under an earlier November 12, 2025 Annex-2 EIA Positive decision for 59,900 birds per cycle across 5 coops (just 100 birds below the 60,000-bird Annex-1 threshold), the company seeks to add 6 coops (+102,020 birds per cycle) to reach a final capacity of 161,920 birds per cycle across 11 coops and 16,608 m2 of enclosed area. Submitted under Annex-1 Item 17-d of the EIA Regulation with a project cost of 106,539,200 TL, the site lies 2.5 km from the nearest settlement (Kaymazyayla) and 7.5 km from the nearest Qualified Natural Protection Area. Primary environmental and legal concerns in the excerpted pages include potential threshold-splitting (proje parçalama), an internal contradiction between DSİ's statement that the site lies within the Kaymaz Irrigation Area and the report's Sensitive Areas table denying the presence of irrigated agricultural lands, and reliance on tanker/carboy water supply for 44.39 m3/day alongside an unexplainedly low 0.09 m3/day industrial wastewater estimate.
