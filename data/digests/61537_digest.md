**Summary (EN):** The proposed 'Non-Hazardous Plastic Waste Pyrolysis Plant (10 tons/day)' project by Seven Kolonya Parfümeri San. ve Tic. Ltd. Şti. in Karabük Organized Industrial Zone (Parcel 102/196) involves processing non-hazardous plastic waste via thermal pyrolysis within an existing 1,750 m2 indoor facility. The project aims to produce pyrolytic oil and carbon black, operating 300 days a year. While the Project Presentation File outlines basic environmental management and compliance with national regulations, it lacks robust baseline multi-seasonal ecological surveys and comprehensive cumulative regional impact assessments.
