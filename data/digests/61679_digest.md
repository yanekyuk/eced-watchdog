**Summary (EN):** The Housing Development Administration of Turkey (TOKİ) proposes constructing 3,850 residential units, 108 shops, a primary school, a family health center, and a mosque across multiple zones in Sazlıbosna Neighborhood, Arnavutköy District, Istanbul [s. 1, 2]. Spanning a ÇED area of 314,875.46 m2 with a total construction footprint of 459.757,02 m2, the project carries a total investment cost of approximately 9.677,885,271,00 TRY [s. 10, 26]. The project site is located within the Sazlıdere Dam drinking-water catchment area, posing critical environmental risks regarding regional hydrogeology and bird migration routes [s. 78, 80].
