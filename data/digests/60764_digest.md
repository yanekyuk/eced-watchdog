Dosya kapsamında tesise kabul edilen süt miktarında artış olmayacağı belirtilerek ÇED Yönetmeliği Ek-2 kapsamında ÇED Gerekli Değil/ÇED Olumlu kararı verilmiştir. **Summary (EN):** Sütaş Süt Ürünleri A.Ş. plans to install an additional 15 MWt steam boiler and a 7.5 MWt indirect air heater at its existing dairy processing plant in Tire, İzmir, with a total project cost of 91,031,862 TL, bringing the total installed thermal capacity to 42.012 MWt. Although the filing claims no increase in raw milk processing capacity, an 'Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) Not Required / Positive (Annex-2)' decision was issued. Key technical concerns include the complete omission of baseline flora and fauna field surveys under the assumption of an Organized Industrial Zone (OSB) location, alongside proximity to the Küçük Menderes River and significant cumulative capacity expansions.
