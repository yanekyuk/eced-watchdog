Faaliyet kapsamında yoğun kimyasal kullanımı, asidik atıksu oluşumu ve hava emisyonları öngörülmekte olup, yakın çevredeki hassas ekosistemler ve altyapı entegrasyonu açısından çevresel riskler taşımaktadır.

**Summary (EN):** The Project Presentation Dossier for the anodizing plant proposed by Kafkas Aluminum in Başakşehir, Istanbul, has received an 'EIA Positive (Annex-2)' decision from the local governorship. The facility operates in a 160 square meter indoor area and plans to increase its total bath capacity from 31.5 cubic meters to 51.02 cubic meters. The project involves intensive chemical consumption, acidic wastewater generation, and air emissions, presenting environmental risks regarding surrounding ecosystems and infrastructure integration.
