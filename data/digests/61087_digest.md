**Summary (EN):** The General Directorate of Energy Affairs proposes the G26-Denizli Solar Power Plant (36 MWm / 30 MWe) covering 44.8 hectares on parcels 3/116 and 379/1 in Yukarı and Gürpınar neighborhoods of Çivril district, Denizli, within a designated Renewable Energy Resource Area (YEKA) [s. 1, 8]. According to the Annex-1 EIA Application File, the facility will install 72,000 bifacial monocrystalline silicon modules (500 W each) to generate 57.6 GWh/year over a 49-year economic life, with a 24-month construction phase (50 staff) and 10 operational staff [s. 2, 9, 11, 13]. Currently, 98.53% of the site is Class VII shrubland on brown forest soils and 1.47% is Class III dry farmland within the Büyük Menderes Basin, situated in a high seismic zone (PGA 475: 0.3 g - 0.4 g) near seasonal dry streambeds [s. 28, 31, 32, 37, 39, 42]. Primary environmental gaps in the application file include the complete deferral of flora and fauna field surveys to the subsequent EIA Report stage and an internal contradiction regarding whether the site falls inside a Wildlife Reintroduction Area [s. 25, 38].
