**Summary (EN):** Uniformagro Tarım Ltd. Şti. has planned a capacity increase for its liquid and solid fertilizer mixing, filling, packaging, and storage facility located in Aşağıoba Neighborhood, Döşemealtı District, Antalya, spanning an area of 1,210 m2. Under the project, the liquid fertilizer capacity will reach 11,520 tons/year, solid fertilizer capacity 6,480 tons/year, and storage volume 262 m3. The project area is situated within the 1st degree Duraliler Protection Area and is located 5,300 meters away from the Kırkgözler Water Springs.
