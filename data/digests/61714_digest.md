Proje kapsamında Alodin-Eloksal Kaplama Hattı, Çinko-Nikel Kaplama Hattı ve alternatif bir hat olmak üzere her biri 126 m³ toplam havuz hacmine sahip 3 hat kurulması planlanmaktadır. **Summary (EN):** The Surface Cleaning, Coating, and Painting Facility project proposed by Mefasan Savunma Sistemleri A.Ş. in the Sincan district of Ankara within the Başkent Organized Industrial Zone aims to process metal parts for the defense industry. The project includes three planned lines (Alodin-Anodizing, Zinc-Nickel, and an alternative line), each with a total tank volume of 126 m³. With an investment cost of 6,150,000 TRY, the facility's project description states that an industrial wastewater treatment plant will not be constructed initially, and wastewater will be transferred to licensed disposal companies for the first six months.
