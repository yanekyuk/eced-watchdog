**Summary (EN):** Taha Ozkan proposes a capacity expansion of an existing broiler poultry farm on parcel 124/40 (19,430.98 m2) in Ozkoy Neighborhood, Gudul District, Ankara, adding a two-story poultry house (1,920 m2 footprint) to raise capacity from 50,000 to 119,120 birds per cycle across six cycles per year under Annex-1 Item 17-d of the EIA Regulation [s. 1, 2, 8]. The site lies 688 m from the nearest farmhouse and 2,051 m from Ozkoy village center, immediately adjacent to another 50,000-bird broiler facility on parcel 124/41 [s. 9, 11]. Primary environmental and legal concerns identified in the reviewed EIA Application File include the judicial annulment of the 1/100,000-scale Ankara Environmental Plan, an uncompleted forest-status entry in the Annex-5 sensitive areas checklist and missing Law 5403 agricultural clearance for a parcel titled partly as arable land, unbuilt wastewater/mortality pit units committed under the 2014 decision, and the absence of completed field-based flora-fauna, groundwater, and cumulative modeling baselines [s. 8, 10, 11, 15, 43, 45, 71, 73].
