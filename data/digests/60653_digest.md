**Summary (EN):** Ahmet Yıldırım proposes to construct and operate a broiler chicken raising facility with a capacity of 40,000 birds per period on Plot 140, Parcel 17 in Pulutlu Village, Central District, Elazığ Province. The project received a 'CED Not Required' (or equivalent Ek-2 approval) decision from the local Governorate. Occupying 2,381 square meters of a 5,000 square meter parcel, the facility will conduct six production cycles per year. The filing indicates the site is designated as agricultural land on the 1/100,000 scale Environmental Plan, water will be sourced from a planned groundwater well and off-site supplies, and wastewater will be managed via a sealed septic tank.
