**Summary (EN):** Tekbir Madencilik proposes an open-pit bauxite quarry using blasting and a mobile crushing-screening plant with a capacity of 600,000 tons/year across a 363.33 ha EIA area (within a 1,500.20 ha license area) in Korucuk and Kızılaliler neighborhoods of Anamur district, Mersin [s. 1, 6, 7]. Located entirely within lands designated as "Forest Area" in the 1/100,000 Environmental Plan, the project site lies 440 m from the nearest settlement (Korucuk) and overlaps in license boundaries with the Korucuk Tepe 1st Degree Archaeological Site [s. 8, 45, 46]. As an Annex-1 EIA Application File, core technical and ecological assessments—including reserve and lifespan calculations, blasting impacts, noise modeling, and flora-fauna surveys—have not yet been completed and are deferred to the forthcoming EIA Report [s. 10, 12, 31, 54]. Notably, Anamur Municipality previously issued a negative opinion citing road-use impacts and lack of alternative routes, and the current filing exhibits internal contradictions and boilerplate omissions [s. 6, 11, 45, 80].
