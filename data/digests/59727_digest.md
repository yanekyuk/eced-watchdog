**Summary (EN):** The planned limestone quarry and crushing-screening plant project by Bursa Metropolitan Municipality in Kıran Işıklar Neighborhood, Keles District, Bursa Province, covers 24.75 hectares within a 44.4-hectare permit area. The project aims to extract 500,000 tons of limestone per year, processing 395,000 tons of it in the crushing plant. The project site is located within forest and agricultural lands, as well as the long-distance protection zone of the Çınarcık Dam.
