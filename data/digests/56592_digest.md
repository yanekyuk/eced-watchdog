**Summary (EN):** Yelten Madencilik ve Ticaret A.Ş. Malkara Branch proposes a Mining Waste (Slurry) Landfill Facility on an 11,880 m2 area in Sarnıç Neighborhood, Malkara District, Tekirdağ Province, for which an 'EIA Positive (Annex-2)' decision has been issued. The project cost is 9,202,515.60 TL, aiming to dispose of 1,033 tons/year of slurry waste generated from the existing coal enrichment plant. The project site is located on parcel no. 7, classified as agricultural land within the Tekirdağ İbrice Great Plain Protection Area.
