**Summary (EN):** The proposed 20.07-hectare Group I-A Borrow Pit project (ER: 3552407) by Kocaeli Metropolitan Municipality in Balçık Village, Gebze, Kocaeli, involves extracting 100,000 tons/year of material within forested lands and the Ömerli Drinking Water Basin long-distance protection zone.
