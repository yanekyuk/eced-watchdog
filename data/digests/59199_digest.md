By adding a two-story coop (Block B, 3,300 m2) and a one-story coop (Block C, 1,650 m2) to the currently under-construction Block A (3,300 m2, which received an 'EIA Not Required' decision on 07.03.2025), total indoor area will reach 8,250 m2 and capacity will increase to 148,500 broilers per cycle across 5 to 6 cycles per year [s. 12, 13, 15]. Key environmental and methodological stakes in the examined pages include unmeasured groundwater levels and conflicting groundwater flow-direction claims, discrepancies in baseline capacity and working-hour figures, and no listed expansion of manure, carcass, or septic containment units to match the increased bird capacity [s. 12, 15, 80, 85].
