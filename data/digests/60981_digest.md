**Summary (EN):** Karesi Polyester ve Petrokimya San. A.Ş. plans a capacity increase for its existing Pet Polyester Chips, Textured Yarn, and Weaving Plant located on plots 6 and 7 in block 1483 within the Bursa Organized Industrial Zone in Nilüfer, Bursa, covering a total area of 84,281.87 m2. The project aims to increase production capacity up to 175,000 tons per year through machinery modernization without any new construction or combustion systems. The Bursa Governorship issued a 'Environmental Impact Assessment Not Required / Positive' decision pursuant to Article 17 of the EIA Regulation.
