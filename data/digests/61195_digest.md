**Summary (EN):** Tarık Şengül proposes an olive oil production, processing, and packaging facility on plot 1773, parcel 2 in Işıklı Neighborhood, Mudanya District, Bursa Province, for which the Bursa Governorship issued a 'Environmental Impact Assessment Not Required' decision. The facility will utilize an existing 459 square meter indoor area with a processing capacity of 9,000 tons per year, operating 100 days annually across 24-hour shifts. The project adopts a 2-phase olive oil extraction system, and the filing addresses stream protection buffers along Çınarlı Stream and wastewater management measures.
