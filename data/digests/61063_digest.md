**Summary (EN):** The proposed project by 3F Biyokimya to produce 14,131.2 tons/year of neutral oil from vegetable and animal crude fats in Kemalpaşa, İzmir, has received a ÇED Gerekli Değil decision. Operating with 2 staff members, the facility will generate chemical neutralization byproducts (soap stock) and discharge domestic wastewater to the municipal sewer system. Situated within an industrial zone according to the environmental master plan, the project omitted baseline flora and fauna field studies.
