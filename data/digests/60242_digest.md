**Summary (EN):** Polen Turizm Yatırımları A.Ş. proposes a capacity expansion of the Seaden Signature Resort & Spa tourism accommodation facility on a 21,824.47 m2 site (Block 870, Parcels 2-3) in Çolaklı Neighborhood, Manavgat District, Antalya Province, increasing capacity from 248 rooms and 562 beds to 371 rooms and 945 beds [s. 1, 17]. With a declared project cost of 287,081,040.00 TL, an estimated 2-year construction phase employing 500 workers, and an operational workforce of 350, the expansion triggers Annex-1 Item 40 (hotels with 250 rooms or more) and Article 20 of the Turkish EIA Regulation [s. 2, 17, 18]. The site lies entirely within the Belek Special Environmental Protection Area (ÖÇK), approximately 200 m from the coastline and 145 m from the nearest residence [s. 20]. Key environmental and legal concerns apparent in the available excerpts include potential threshold evasion and staged permitting (an Annex-2 EIA Positive decision for 248 rooms—just two rooms below the Annex-1 threshold—issued on 06.05.2026 followed within weeks by a 03.06.2026 site visit for an expansion to 371 rooms), the placement of 70 new rooms in the first basement level near the sea, and internal contradictions regarding whether a construction phase exists [s. 11, 17, 19, 20].
