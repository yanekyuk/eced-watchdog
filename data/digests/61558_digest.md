**Summary (EN):** The proposed broiler poultry breeding facility in Manisa, Köprübaşı, Döğüşören Village, parcel 518, features 2 houses with a capacity of 57,040 birds per period and a total investment value of 31,554,200 TRY [s. 2, 6]. The project site is located within agricultural and olive grove lands, as well as the Döğüşören Dam irrigation zone and a groundwater protection basin [s. 10, 18, 20]. The filing relies on literature-only flora and fauna assessments and presents significant technical gaps regarding irrigation safety, flood risks, and cumulative odor impacts [s. 18, 19, 57].
