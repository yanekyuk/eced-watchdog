**Summary (EN):** Gündoğdu Dairy Industry Dış Tic. A.Ş. proposes a dairy processing capacity expansion across adjacent parcels 104/8 and 104/13 within the Salihli Organized Industrial Zone in Manisa Province [s. 2, 7, 10]. Although parcel 8 holds a 600 t/day EIA Positive decision (currently processing 150.45 t/day) and parcel 13 holds a 90 t/day EIA Not Required decision (currently processing 3 t/day), the project adds 600 t/day on parcel 13 to bring combined permitted capacity to 1,290 t/day with an investment cost of 471,967,584 TL under Annex-1 Item 20 [s. 2, 7, 8, 9]. Primary environmental and legal concerns identified in the excerpted sections include a direct internal contradiction regarding groundwater wells and aquifers, scientifically impossible 'visual monitoring' ('gözle izleme') commitments for noise and air emissions, copy-paste errors referencing Aydın Province and Aydın Municipality, literature-only biodiversity lists, and failure to evaluate olive groves within the statutory 3 km radius under Law No. 3573 [s. 44, 47, 57, 63, 67, 92].
