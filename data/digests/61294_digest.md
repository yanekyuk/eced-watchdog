**Summary (EN):** Gulle Entegre Tekstil proposes to install a single 4.80 MWm auxiliary wind turbine on parcel 180/373 in Kirkgoz Neighborhood, Ergene District, Tekirdag Province, to supply its existing 19.282 MWm four-unit gas-engine cogeneration plant. Following a project revision, the turbine rotor diameter was enlarged from 138 m to 155 m and the plant area expanded from 27 ha to 30.90 ha, bringing total combined mechanical capacity to 24.0820 MWm (18.784 MWe). The site overlaps areas designated as First-Priority (Absolute) Agricultural Protection Areas and Excessive Groundwater Extraction Areas under the 1/25,000 provincial environmental plan, while the nearest residential settlement, Ulas Neighborhood, lies 693 m from the license boundary.

İlgililere ve kamuoyuna duyurulur.
