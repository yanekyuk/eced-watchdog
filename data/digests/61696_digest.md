**Summary (EN):** Proposed by Hayri Ögelman Madencilik A.Ş. in Bursa province, Orhaneli district, Çeki neighborhood, the project aims to establish a high-carbon ferrochromium and aggregate production facility with an annual capacity of 43,000 tons of ferrochromium and 40,000 tons of aggregate on a 95,624.21 m2 forest land. With an investment cost of 2.355.500.000 TL, the industrial facility falls under the EIA Regulation Annex-2 list and poses significant environmental risks regarding intensive water consumption, waste generation, and pressures on the forest ecosystem. The report reveals that flora and fauna assessments are largely based on literature reviews and cumulative impacts are inadequately evaluated.
