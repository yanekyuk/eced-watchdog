**Summary (EN):** Ark Statik Boya Makine İnş. Tarım Hayvancılık San. Tic. Ltd. Şti. plans to establish a Surface Treatment Pre-Treated Static Powder Coating and Varnishing Facility on parcel 8, block 22735 in Büyükkayacık Neighborhood, Selçuklu District, Konya Province. The project will utilize 6,000 m2 of indoor space within a 9,596.21 m2 plot, operating surface treatment and chromate plating pools with a total capacity of 40.5 m3. Although the facility is located within an organized industrial zone and involves no construction phase, strict administrative and technical oversight is required regarding hazardous waste management, air emission filtration systems, and wastewater discharge compliance.
