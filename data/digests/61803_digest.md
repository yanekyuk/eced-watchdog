**Summary (EN):** Etu Yapı Ticaret Limited Şirketi has submitted an Annex-1 EIA Application File for the Almaris Resort Hotel Capacity Increase project on Parcel 456/4 (7,908.89 m²) in Cikcikli Neighborhood, Alanya, Antalya [p. 2, 6]. Following a construction fire in Block B after obtaining a prior 'EIA Not Required' decision on 15 October 2024 for 245 rooms and 490 beds, the developer proposes subdividing units to reach 495 rooms, 1,292 beds, and a total construction area of 26,288.50 m² at an estimated cost of 1,421,934,750.00 TL [p. 2, 6, 11]. The site is located 20 meters as the crow flies from the nearest residence and approximately 1.4 km from the coastline [p. 6, 50]. Key environmental and legal stakes identified in the reviewed excerpts include zoning contradictions between residential designations in local plans and a large-scale hotel complex, omission of pool, laundry, kitchen, and SPA process waters from the 474.4 m³/day operational wastewater calculation, a strictly literature-based flora table, and unanswered forest and agricultural land items in the sensitive areas checklist [p. 8, 10, 19, 50, 51, 55].
