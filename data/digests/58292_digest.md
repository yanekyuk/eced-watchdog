**Summary (EN):** The Liquid, Pasteurized, and Powdered Egg Production Facility planned by Egg Valley Gıda Sanayi ve Ticaret A.Ş. Aksaray Branch in Aksaray Merkez Erenler OSB has a processing capacity of 210 tons/day of shell eggs on plot 144/1. With an investment value of 1,294,061,131.28 TL, the project will expand existing buildings to a total gross construction area of 21,590 m² and include a 250 m³/day industrial wastewater treatment plant. Generating approximately 200 m³/day of wastewater, treated effluents will be discharged into the organized industrial zone sewerage system.
