**Summary (EN):** Proposed by the Housing Development Administration of Turkey (TOKİ) in Çünür Neighborhood, Central District, Isparta Province on Plot 111/3, the project covers a 2.92-hectare EIA area involving 405 residential units, 3 caretaker flats, two 5-shop commercial centers, and 1 mosque. With a total construction area of 40,682.39 m² and an investment cost of 856,364,309.50 TL, the project anticipates 80 workers during construction and a population of 1,852 residents during operation. The project site is located 147 meters away from the nearest settlement and sits within an alluvial-karstic aquifer transition zone.
