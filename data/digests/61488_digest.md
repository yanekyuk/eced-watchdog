Yeraltı ve yerüstü su kaynakları, koku, gübre yönetimi ve tarım dışı arazi kullanımı açısından önemli çevresel riskler barındırmaktadır.

**Summary (EN):** Vahap Aydoğdu proposes to construct a broiler poultry breeding facility with a capacity of 55,900 broilers per period in 2 barns on plot 3, block 119, in Arpacı Neighborhood, Köprübaşı District, Manisa Province. The facility will occupy a total construction area of 3,391.5 square meters within a 23,921.22 square meter land parcel. The project site is located within a designated forest area according to the 1/100,000 scale Environmental Plan and falls within the Long-Distance Protection Area of the Gördes Dam, which supplies drinking water to İzmir. The project entails significant environmental risks regarding groundwater and surface water resources, odor management, manure disposal, and non-agricultural land use.
