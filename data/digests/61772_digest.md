Proje Sahibi Ekinci Kurumsal Yapı İnş. San. Tic. Ltd. Şti. ile Yılmaz Sezgin İş Ortaklığı tarafından hazırlanan Proje Tanıtım Dosyası'na göre yatırım bedeli 485.000.000 TL olarak belirlenmiştir. **Summary (EN):** The Housing and Business Center Project, planned by Ekinci Kurumsal Yapı and Yılmaz Sezgin Partnership in Evren Neighborhood, Merkez District, Siirt, comprises 344 residential units and 33 commercial spaces on a 21,083.42 square meter plot. The total project cost is stated as 485,000,000 TL, with 40 construction workers and approximately 1,440 residents during the operation phase. While designated as an urban development area in the regional plan, the Project Introduction File exhibits generalized flora-fauna literature lists and lacks comprehensive pre-project baseline verifications.
