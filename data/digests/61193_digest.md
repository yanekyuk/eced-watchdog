**Summary (EN):** Maxion İnci Jant Sanayi A.Ş. Aluminum Wheel Plant Branch proposes adding a Non-Hazardous Waste Recovery and Hazardous Waste Pre-Treatment Process with a total capacity of 87.555 tons/day within its existing 72,058.03 m² parcel (52,000 m² enclosed area) in Manisa Organized Industrial Zone Part III (Yunusemre, Kayapınar Mah., Block 3311, Parcel 18). With a declared investment cost of 65,595,465 TL and no new building construction, the project includes installing a scrap aluminum wheel crushing unit, recovering swarf in an existing melting furnace, and physically de-oiling hazardous swarf via centrifugation without solvents until oil content drops below 1%. The additions will not alter existing wheel manufacturing capacity, will add 3 workers to the current 955-person workforce, and will operate 300 days/year across three shifts (24 hours/day). Key environmental considerations involve air emissions from high-temperature (760-1,000 °C) melting and Sb/Na refining of scrap and de-oiled swarf, verification of the <1% residual oil threshold, and the cumulative implications of numerous fragmented EIA decisions issued for the site since 2001.
