**Summary (EN):** RNC Avrasya Enerji Tedarik Depolama Ticaret A.Ş. proposes a 100 MWm / 100 MWe solar power plant (GES) coupled with a 100 MWe / 100 MWh lithium iron phosphate (LFP) battery electricity storage facility across 174.86 hectares of parcel 158/132 in Obruk Village, Bor District, Niğde Province [s. 1, s. 15, s. 30, s. 76].
