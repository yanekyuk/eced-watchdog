**Summary (EN):** Polifarma İlaç San. ve Tic. A.Ş. Çorlu Branch proposes adding oligonucleotide-based and chemical synthesis Active Pharmaceutical Ingredient (API) production as well as expanded parenteral solution manufacturing within its existing facility in the Ergene-1 Organized Industrial Zone (Block 461, Parcel 25), Ergene District, Tekirdağ [s. 1, s. 12, s. 13]. With a projected investment of 800,000,000 TL, the project adds 3.00 kg/year of oligonucleotide API, 30.00 kg/year of chemical synthesis API, 6,804.00 tons/year of liquid pharmaceuticals, and 86,400.00 L/year of vial/syringe/cartridge parenteral solutions alongside the existing 61,689.60 tons/year serum output [s. 2, s. 13]. Classified under EIA Regulation Annex-1 Articles 33 and 29, the expansion requires no new land outside the existing industrial parcel, yet key environmental stakes center on the absence of solvent recovery, volatile organic compound (VOC) emissions, industrial wastewater pre-treatment, and hazardous chemical/waste management [s. 2, s. 14, s. 57]. Within the supplied excerpts, key baseline limitations include deferred spatial proximity verification for nearby protected sites and reliance on prior regional reports and literature for flora and fauna characterization [s. 79, s. 80, s. 81].
