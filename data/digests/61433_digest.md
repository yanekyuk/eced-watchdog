**Summary (EN):** The General Directorate of Energy Affairs proposes the R26-Manisa Wind Power Plant (34 turbines of 7 MWm each, totaling 238 MWm / 235 MWe) spanning Demirci district in Manisa and Simav district in Kütahya [s. 1, 8, 11]. Of the 93,385,363 m2 EIA study area, 86.1% is classified as forest, 5.0% pasture, 4.5% dry agriculture, and 4.4% shrubland, while the site lies 2 km from Demirci town center and 25 meters from Gelinuçtu Nature Park [s. 8, 47]. The filing documents that an active fault (Demirci Fault, with PGA 475 ground acceleration of 0.4 to 0.5 g) as well as active and historical landslides intersect the EIA area, alongside multiple local streams [s. 35, 36, 40]. At this application stage, no baseline field surveys for flora, fauna, or avian migration are presented, with detailed ecological studies deferred to the subsequent full EIA Report [s. 29, 54].
