**Summary (EN):** Karayolları 5th Regional Directorate plans to establish an Asphalt Plant with a capacity of 340 tons/hour in Mersin Province, Gülnar District, Mollaömerli Village, Plot 102, Parcel 516 [s. 1, 7]. The project envisages an annual production of 816,000 tons of hot mix asphalt on a designated area of 16,535.79 m2 [s. 4, 14]. Biodiversity assessments within the project area identified local endemic Alchemilla paracompactilis and endemic Turanecio jurineifolius plant species [s. 53]. An 'EIA Positive (Annex-2 Scope)' decision was issued by the Mersin Governorate [s. s. GİRİŞ].
