**Summary (EN):** MAV Elektrik Uretim Sanayi ve Ticaret A.S. proposes to construct and operate the Gerze Wind Power Plant (8 turbines, 60 MWm/48 MWe installed capacity) integrated with a 48 MWh/48 MWe Lithium-Ion Battery Energy Storage System across a 2,029.09-hectare license area in the Boyabat and Gerze districts of Sinop Province. Classified under Annex-1 Article 41 of the Turkish EIA Regulation, the finalized EIA report has been opened for public comment under Article 14 following the second Review and Evaluation Commission (IDK) meeting. Based on the supplied excerpts, the turbines, storage containers, and switchyard sit on agricultural, forest, and scrubland parcels under the 1/100,000 Environmental Plan, with the nearest inhabited dwelling located approximately 498 meters northwest of turbine T6. Indexes and figure lists further indicate the presence of archaeological sites within the license boundary, proximity to Tirnali Stream, and grid tie-in to the Hamsi WPP-Boyabat line, underscoring the need to scrutinize full baseline ecology, land-use permits, and cumulative impacts.
