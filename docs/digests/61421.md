**Summary (EN):** The proposed Broiler Poultry Breeding Facility by Ali Faik İşmar in Uşak Province, Ulubey District, Büyükkayalı Village (Plot 183, Parcel 4) covers a 17,213.31 m2 agricultural plot with two poultry houses totaling 3,500 m2 and a capacity of 59,500 broilers per period. Located within an area designated as 'Agricultural Lands' in the 1/100,000 scale Environmental Plan, the project entails potential environmental risks including odor emissions, manure management, wastewater disposal, and water sourcing. The filing relies strictly on literature reviews for flora and fauna without conducting mandatory multi-season baseline field surveys.
