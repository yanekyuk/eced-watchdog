Tunceli İl Özel İdaresi tarafından yürütülmesi planlanan HÜİB: 62/2026-01 (ER:3542248) Numaralı II-A Grubu Kalker Ocağı Kapasite Artışı ve İlave Kırma Eleme Tesisi Projesi, 12,58 hektarlık ruhsat ve ÇED alanını kapsamaktadır. **Summary (EN):** The proposed project involves a capacity increase for a Class II-A limestone quarry and the addition of a crushing and screening plant by the Tunceli Special Provincial Administration in Mazgirt district, Tunceli province, covering an area of 12.58 hectares. The project anticipates an annual production of 390,000 tons within pasture and treasury lands overlapping with forest ecosystems, located approximately 1.2 kilometers from the nearest settlement of Kukurik (Aktarla) village.
