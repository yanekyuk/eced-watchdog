**Summary (EN):** Kalyon Enerji Yatırımları A.Ş. proposes to construct and operate the Ordu Wind Power Plant (33 turbines, 99.6 MWm/99.6 MWe), an Energy Storage Facility (99.6 MWe/99.6 MWh), and a switchyard across Mesudiye (Ordu), Koyulhisar (Sivas), and Reşadiye (Tokat) districts [s. 1, 2, 25]. Evaluated under Annex-1 Article 41/a of the EIA Regulation with a stated project cost of 2,722,198,748.86 TL, the project has received a positive EIA decision [s. 2]. Construction involves clearing 64,809.62 m³ (103,695.17 tonnes) of topsoil across turbine platforms, storage, and switchyard areas, alongside constructing approximately 10,750 m of new 10 m-wide access roads [s. 475, 476]. Key environmental and legal stakes include turbine siting within forest areas—three of which fall in productive and regeneration forests—as well as pasture (yaylak/mera) and agricultural lands, a provincial boundary and environmental plan threshold discrepancy, and a floristic baseline study conducted solely in July 2025 without covering the entire project flora [s. 25, 29, 39, 63, 1780].
