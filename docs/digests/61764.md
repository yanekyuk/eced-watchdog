**Summary (EN):** Felix Madencilik plans to operate an 83-room, 128-bed tourism accommodation facility within an existing 1,200.17 m2 building located in Yenişehir district, Mersin (Parcel 618/13). The facility, which previously served as a student dormitory and requires no construction phase, is projected to accommodate 10 staff and 107 guests, generating 28.197 m3/day of domestic wastewater. Given its close proximity of 8 meters to the nearest residential building and its location in a dense urban matrix with literature-only baseline data, the project presents notable urban and environmental compatibility concerns.
