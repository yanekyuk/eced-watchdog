**Summary (EN):** Medmar Mermer Madencilik A.Ş. plans to operate a marble quarry on a 17.05-hectare ÇED area within a 99.52-hectare licensed zone located in Taşkale Village, Central District, Karaman Province. The project anticipates an annual total material extraction of 150,000 m3 (comprising 135,000 m3 of waste/rubble and 15,000 m3 of block marble) with an estimated project life of 26 years.
