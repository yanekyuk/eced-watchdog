**Summary (EN):** The Çolak-1 Storage Solar Power Plant and Electrical Storage Facility project (19 MWe/19 MWm, 21.41 hectares), proposed by Çolak Energy Inc. in Sarınasuhlar Neighborhood, Yunusemre District, Manisa Province, has been reviewed. The project area is entirely designated as agricultural land on the 1/100.000 scale Izmir-Manisa Environmental Plan and intersects with closed groundwater operation zones according to DSİ statements.
