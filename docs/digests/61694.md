**Summary (EN):** The State Highways 4th Regional Directorate proposes the operation of the Shéhahmetli Group I-(A) borrow pit project with an annual capacity of 720,000 tons within an 8.85-hectare ÇED area located in Polatlı, Ankara [s. 6, 16]. The project involves unblasted open-pit extraction of limestone to supply highway construction and maintenance works in the region [s. 11, 19]. The entire project footprint consists of agricultural land and is situated approximately 20 meters away from seasonal dry stream beds such as Özdere [s. 6, 7].
