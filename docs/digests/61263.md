Tesiste yıllık 24.000 ton metal döküm kapasitesi (hurda çelik ve pik demir) planlanmakta olup, 3D kum baskı teknolojisi ve elektrikli indüksiyon ocakları kullanılacaktır. **Summary (EN):** The proposed Metal Casting and Non-Hazardous Waste Recovery Facility by TIRON DÖKÜM A.Ş. in Edirne Province, Uzunköprü District, Atatürk Organized Industrial Zone (Plot 467, Parcel 96), will span a total construction area of 19,135 m2 on a 30,000 m2 land plot. The facility plans a total metal casting capacity of 24,000 tons/year utilizing 3D sand printing technology and electric induction furnaces. The project site is located 560 meters away from the nearest residential settlement in Atatürk Neighborhood and 2.5 kilometers from Kırkkavak Village.
