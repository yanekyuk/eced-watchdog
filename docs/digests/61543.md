**Summary (EN):** Eti Maden İşletmeleri Genel Müdürlüğü proposes the Bandırma New Waste Storage Facilities project in Çalışkanlar Neighborhood, Bandırma District, Balıkesir Province, with an estimated cost of 920,000,000 TL, to replace the existing Ağıldere Tailings Dam scheduled for closure by 2026 [s. 1, 2, 9]. The project comprises three waste storage facilities across two EIA areas with a total storage volume of 1,618,544 m³, an annual waste input of 499,333.3 m³ of boron process tailings and wash water, and an operational lifespan of approximately 3.24 years [s. 9, 10, 16]. The proposed site encompasses land plots, olive groves, agricultural areas, pastures, and forest lands within the Manyas Lake Sub-basin of the Susurluk Basin, which drains toward the Manyas Bird Paradise protected under Ramsar and National Park statuses [s. 10, 78]. Key environmental and technical issues in the filing include discrepancies between hazardous waste codes and Class II landfill / non-hazardous NACE classifications, permeable fractured carbonate zones and springs south of the site, and a fauna baseline relying entirely on literature due to zero field detections [s. 2, 20, 42, 77, 78].
