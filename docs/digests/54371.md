**Summary (EN):** AYGÜNGES Elektrik Üretim A.Ş. proposes the Üyük Storage-Integrated Wind Power Plant in Söğüt District, Bilecik Province (near Sırhoca, Gündüzbey, Savcıbey, and Hayriye villages), comprising four 7.5 MW turbines (30 MWm/30 MWe total) and a 30 MWe/30 MWh lithium-ion battery energy storage facility [s. 1, s. 20]. Key environmental and planning stakes identified in the excerpts include overlap with a Group IV gold-silver mining license and village drinking water and sewage pipelines [s. 29], a project impact zone restricted to a 250 m construction-noise boundary [s. 60], and incomplete parcel/ownership records for several project units [s. 61].
