Proje alanı ham toprak vasfında olup Antalya-Burdur-Isparta 1/100.000 Ölçekli Çevre Düzeni Planı'na göre tarım ve çayır-mera kullanımında kalmaktadır. **Summary (EN):** The proposed GEPAT Storage GES project by GEPAT Elektrik Enerjisi Tedarik A.Ş., located in Burdur province, Karamanlı district, Mürseller village (Parcel 162/38), encompasses a 10 MWm/10 MWe solar power plant spanning 10.09 hectares and a 40 MWh/10 MWe lithium-ion battery energy storage system covering 0.23 hectares. The project site is classified as raw land and falls under agricultural and pasture designations according to the 1/100,000 scale Environmental Plan. An Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) Waiver / Approval (Annex-2 Positive) decision was issued by the local governorate, subject to future non-agricultural land use permits and compliance with Pasture Law provisions.
