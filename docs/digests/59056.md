**Summary (EN):** The General Directorate of Mineral Research and Exploration (MTA) plans to drill one geothermal exploration well with a depth of 2,750 meters within license area no. 1693 in Buruncuk Village, Menemen District, İzmir Province. Flora and fauna assessments in the PTD file rely solely on literature data, and required mitigation measures and land permission processes must be rigorously audited under Council of State case law.
