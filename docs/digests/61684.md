**Summary (EN):** The proposed Ceramic Production Plant Capacity Increase project by Vibroser Porselen Karo Sanayi ve Ticaret A.Ş. in Eskişehir, İnönü, Yenice Neighborhood, Plot 253, Parcel 54, aims to raise the production capacity from 975.55 tons/year to 46,464.264 tons/year. Located on an existing 16,335 m2 industrial parcel within agricultural lands designated by the 1/100,000 scale Environmental Plan, the project involves adding a new kiln and technological upgrades without any new construction. The nearest settlement is Bozüyük Akpınar village houses at a distance of 2 km, while the D-650 highway runs 30 meters away from the site boundary.
