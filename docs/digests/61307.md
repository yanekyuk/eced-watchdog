**Summary (EN):** The proposed hotel project by Emin Ak, with a capacity of 103 rooms (206 beds), is planned to be constructed on a 1,542.77 m2 land parcel (Plot 466, Block 14) in Şekerpınar2 Neighborhood, Çayırova District, Kocaeli Province, with a total closed construction area of 6,783.62 m2. The total investment cost is calculated as 484.461.737 TL, and the local governorate has issued a 'Environmental Impact Assessment Not Required' decision under Annex-2 of the EIA Regulation. Located within urban residential and commercial zones, the primary environmental risks of the project stem from domestic/hazardous wastes, wastewater generation, and construction/operational noise and traffic loads.
