**Summary (EN):** The Broiler Poultry Breeding Facility proposed by Mahmut Önen in Niğde province, Ulukışla district, Hürriyet neighborhood, plot 176 parcel 5, features a capacity of 58,680 broilers per period across two poultry houses spanning 3,260 m2 within a 59,150 m2 land plot [s. 1, 7]. The project filing addresses highway transit conditions from the 6th Regional Directorate of Highways, groundwater allocation constraints within the 16/4 Ereğli-Bor sub-basin by DSİ, and sensitive fauna evaluations including Testudo graeca by DKMP [s. 10, 12, 17].
