**Summary (EN):** Eskikale İnşaat A.Ş. plans to establish a 240 tons/hour asphalt plant project on parcel 5, block 142 in Karapınarkavağı village, Mudurnu district, Bolu province [s. 1, 7]. Out of a total parcel area of 16,889.97 square meters, 4.577 square meters are designated as the EIA area, with the nearest residential building located just 80 meters away [s. 8, 60]. The project aims to produce asphalt for highway construction works under the General Directorate of Highways, yet environmental risks including dust emissions, domestic and industrial wastes, and fuel/bitumen storage pose significant concerns for nearby residents and agricultural lands [s. 7, 8, 20].
