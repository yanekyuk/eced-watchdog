**Summary (EN):** ETİ Gıda Sanayi ve Ticaret A.Ş. proposes a simultaneous capacity expansion across its four existing biscuit, chocolate, cake, and cracker plants located within the 75. Yıl Organized Industrial Zone in Odunpazarı, Eskişehir, with a total investment value of 4,729,800,000 TL [s. 1, 2, 9]. Following a Ministry directive dated 30.09.2026 requiring cumulative assessment under a single Annex-1 EIA application, the project proposes adding five new production lines (four in the biscuit plant and one in the cake plant) and expanding operating schedules across all four facilities from 300 days/year and 8 hours/day to 350 days/year and 24 hours/day [s. 9, 10, 11]. Combined annual output across the four plants is projected to rise more than fourfold from roughly 183.0 million kg/year to 767.4 million kg/year, while total employment increases from 5,111 to approximately 5,880 [s. 9, 10, 11, 12]. Although the application file states that all expansion will occur within existing buildings without new construction, it defers quantitative baseline and impact assessments for water consumption, wastewater discharge to the Organized Industrial Zone sewerage system and Sakarya Basin receiving waters (Porsuk Stream and Kanlıpınar Pond), air emissions, traffic, and field flora-fauna surveys to the subsequent full EIA Report while simultaneously asserting the absence of sensitive or endemic species [s. 12, 16, 28, 33, 41, 126].
