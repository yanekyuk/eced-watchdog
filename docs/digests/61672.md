**Summary (EN):** ARAS Salvage Enkaz Kaldırma Acil Müdahale ve Deniz Teknolojileri A.Ş. proposes a Ship Rescue Vessels Berthing Dock project in Kalınburun, Tatlısu Neighborhood, Erdek District, Balıkesir, with an estimated cost of 104,475,300.00 TL following the expiration of an unimplemented 2014 EIA positive decision [s. 2, s. 8, s. 11]. Located seaward of the shoreline, the project covers 7,347.82 m2 in total, comprising 674.70 m2 of coastal fill, 81.12 m2 of existing concrete structures, a 78.36 m dock, and 6,591.99 m2 of leased sea surface for berthing emergency response, salvage, and dredging vessels up to 95 m in length [s. 8, s. 9, s. 12, s. 13]. The entire site lies within the Marmara Sea and Islands Special Environmental Protection Area (ÖÇK), approximately 220 m east of Balta Creek, and in a high-seismic hazard zone (PGA 0.381 g, 1.1 km from a mapped Holocene fault) [s. 9, s. 17, s. 45, s. 46, s. 47]. Because this is an EIA Application File, detailed field surveys and terrestrial/endangered species investigations are deferred to the subsequent EIA Report stage [s. 28, s. 37, s. 54].
