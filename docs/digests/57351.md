Proje kapsamında yılda 800.000 ton açık işletme ve 1.250.000 ton yeraltı işletmesi olmak üzere toplam 2.050.000 ton/yıl tüvenan cevher üretimi hedeflenmektedir. **Summary (EN):** Manolya Madencilik A.Ş. plans to implement a blasting design change, capacity increase, and underground mining method addition within the existing 23.5-hectare EIA-permitted area of operating license number 37001, located in Darılık Village, Aladağ District, Adana Province. The project targets an annual run-of-mine production of 800,000 tons from open pit operations and 1,250,000 tons from underground galleries. Primary environmental concerns include the forest land use status, proximity to the Katran Stream, and location within the Yedigöze Dam Long-Distance Protection Area.
