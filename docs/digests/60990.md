**Summary (EN):** AVES Enerji Yağ ve Gıda Sanayi A.Ş. proposes to construct and operate an integrated soybean oil and protein production complex on a 125,457.15 m² parcel (Block 424, Parcel 67) in Edincik Mahallesi, Bandırma District, Balıkesir Province. The facility is designed to process 3,000 tons/day (990,000 tons/year) of soybeans into 594 tons/day of crude soybean oil, soybean meal, and food-grade soy protein concentrate using hexane and ethanol extraction, supported by a 50 tons/hour coal-fired steam boiler, a back-pressure turbine, and a 10 MWp rooftop solar plant. Primary environmental and legal concerns identified in the reviewed excerpts include the deferral of mandatory agricultural and olive-grove clearances (Laws 5403 and 3573) until after EIA approval, reliance on a single-day autumn field survey supplemented by literature for biodiversity, missing cumulative impact evaluation in the excerpted sections, and the planned discharge of approximately 618 m³/day of treated wastewater into the local seasonal creek system draining toward the Marmara Sea.
