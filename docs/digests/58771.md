**Summary (EN):** Selectron Elektrokimya Sanayi ve Ticaret Limited Şirketi plans to establish a surface cleaning line with a total pool volume of 49,077 cubic meters within its existing wood and steel screw manufacturing facility located in Istanbul Province, Silivri District, Alipaşa Neighborhood, Plot 307, Parcel 5. The project occupies a 18,690 square meter plot and aims to increase total employment to 500 personnel operating in three shifts. While the Project Introduction File addresses alluvial aquifer characteristics and wastewater management, the local zoning status and wastewater infrastructure constraints require careful administrative and technical scrutiny.
