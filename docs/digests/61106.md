**Summary (EN):** The proposed Fuel, LPG, and Dry Cargo Storage/Supply Facility project by İzgi İnşaat Enerji Nakliyat Petrol ve Tarım Ürünleri San. Tic. A.Ş., located in Çöltepe Neighborhood, plots 136, parcels 1 and 2, Bismil District, Diyarbakır Province, is evaluated under Annex-II of the EIA Regulation. The project covers an area of 26,399.31 m² and is situated within 'Agricultural Land and Irrigation Area' designations according to the 1/100,000 scale Environmental Plan. Key environmental and legal risks include its location on agricultural land and its proximity of only 25 meters to the nearest residential settlement in Çöltepe.
