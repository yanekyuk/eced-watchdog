Proje kapsamında inşaat çalışması öngörülmemekte olup, işletme aşamasında günlük azami 5.800 kişi (konaklayanlar, ziyaretçiler ve personel) üzerinden su tüketimi, evsel atık ve atıksu yükleri hesaplanmıştır.

**Summary (EN):** The Project Presentation File (PTD) for the existing Eser Diamond Hotel Spa & Convention Center located in Silivri, Istanbul, proposing an addition of 10 rooms to reach a total capacity of 218 rooms and 496 beds, has been evaluated. The Governorate issued a 'CDN / EIA Positive (Annex-2)' decision for the operational facility. Since no new construction is planned, environmental assessments focus on operational impacts, calculating a daily population load of up to 5,800 individuals including guests, visitors, and staff.
