**Summary (EN):** The proposed Broiler Poultry Breeding Facility by Elif Doğan, located in Adana Province, Kozan District, Postkabasakal Village, Block 188, Parcel 10, has a capacity of 35,764 birds per period on a 7,686.41 m2 deeded plot. Operating for 6 periods per year, the total annual production is planned at 214,584 birds, situated on land classified by the Provincial Directorate of Agriculture and Forestry as Dry Absolute Agricultural Land with agricultural construction permits under Law No. 5403. The Project Introduction File contains commitments regarding groundwater extraction, flood risks, and manure storage, with the nearest residential settlement located 617 meters to the south.
