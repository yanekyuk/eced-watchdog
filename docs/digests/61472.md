Proje alanı, Marmara Denizi ve Adalar Özel Çevre Koruma Bölgesi sınırları içerisinde kalmakta olup kıyı ve deniz ekosistemi üzerindeki kümülatif etkilerin denetlenmesi gerekmektedir.

**Summary (EN):** Turquoise Yat Sanayi A.Ş. plans a floating dock capacity increase at its existing yacht construction, maintenance, and repair facility located in Güzelyalı Neighborhood, Pendik District, Istanbul. The project involves enlarging the dimensions of the existing floating dock originally built in 1999 and revised in 2016 to enhance operational capacity. The project area is situated within the Marmara Sea and Islands Specially Protected Area, requiring rigorous scrutiny of cumulative impacts on the marine ecosystem.
