**Summary (EN):** Turkey Hard Coal Enterprises (T.T.K.) General Directorate proposes to operate a montmorillonite quarry with an annual capacity of 100,000 tons on a 24.82-hectare area within the borders of Yeşilkaya and Karainler Villages in Bartın Province, Merkez District. The open-pit mining activity involves no beneficiation, but the overlap of the project area with forest, agricultural, and ecologically sensitive lands constitutes the primary environmental risk. The Governorate has issued a 'EIA Necessary Not / EIA Positive (Annex-2)' decision for the project filing.
