Toplam 3.115.603.100 TL yatırım bedelli projede 57 adet besi işletmesi kapsamında toplam 14.620 büyükbaş besi hayvanı, günlük 150 büyükbaş kapasiteli mezbaha ve et entegre tesisi, yıllık 100.000 ton kapasiteli yem fabrikası, 1.666,5 kW gücünde biyogaz tesisi ve 1.000 m3/gün kapasiteli atıksu arıtma tesisi yer almaktadır [s. 2, 11, 12]. **Summary (EN):** The Kütahya Provincial Directorate of Agriculture and Forestry proposes to establish and operate the Kütahya Aslanapa Agriculture-Based Specialized Organized Industrial Zone (TDİOSB) and Biogas Facility on a 480,388.3 m2 EIA footprint within an 818,123.99 m2 state-owned parcel in Cumhuriyet Neighborhood, Aslanapa District, Kütahya [s. 1, 11]. With a total investment cost of 3,115,603,100 TL, the integrated complex comprises 57 cattle fattening operations totaling 14,620 head, a slaughterhouse and meat processing plant rated at 150 cattle per day, a 100,000 ton/year feed mill, a 1,666.5 kW biogas plant, and a 1,000 m3/day wastewater treatment plant [s. 2, 11, 12]. The nearest settlement is Aslanapa town center located 1,725 meters southwest of the site, while Yalnızsaray, Çukurca, and Esen villages lie between 2,620 and 2,740 meters away [s. 12]. Although the EIA report was revised to incorporate the biogas plant for manure and waste management, the excerpted sections show that the fauna baseline relies solely on outdated literature, the 1,000 m3/day treatment plant is omitted from the project title, and key hydrogeological, wastewater discharge, and cumulative impact evaluations require full-record verification [s. 6, 11, 26].
