**Summary (EN):** SİDRA KİMYA SAN. ve TİC. A.Ş. proposes a facility for the recovery of hazardous and non-hazardous wastes and the production and storage of inorganic chemicals (strontium compounds, sulfates, molybdates, catalysts, and other inorganic compounds) at İstanbul Tuzla Kimya Sanayicileri Organized Industrial Zone (7079 block, parcel 12) in Tuzla, İstanbul [p. 1, p. 16, p. 45]. Classified under Annex-1 of the EIA Regulation with a project cost of 27,118,764.39 TL and granted an 'EIA Positive' decision, the facility plans to process 31,000 tons/year of raw materials (including wastes from wood, formaldehyde, chemical, and petrochemical industries) to yield 29,000 tons/year of solid final products on a wet-weight basis (~24,800 tons/year dry basis) [p. 2, p. 16, p. 45]. Production is expected to generate approximately 300 tons/year of metal oxides and 300 tons/year of hazardous and non-hazardous wastewater treatment sludges, with products and process wastes stored for up to 6 months [p. 45]. Primary environmental stakes involve hazardous waste and chemical handling/storage safety, air emissions and sludge disposal, and proximity (~4.60 km northwest) to Hacetderesi Nature Park alongside existing industrial loads in the Tuzla OIZ [p. 45, p. 65].
