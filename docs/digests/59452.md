**Summary (EN):** The proposed Lubricant Production project by Arma Geri Dönüşüm Enerji Kimya San. ve Tic. Ltd. Şti. in Başiskele, Kocaeli, will be operated on a 3,100 m² industrial plot with 1,613 m² of closed area. The facility plans to produce 12,824.94 tons/year of lubricating oils and store up to 654.757 m³ of chemical substances. No construction works are planned as the existing industrial building and infrastructure will be utilized.
