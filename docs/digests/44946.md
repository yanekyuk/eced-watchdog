**Summary (EN):** An Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) approval decision has been issued by the Governorate for the Broiler Chicken Breeding Facility project planned by Şeyma Doğru in Adana Province, Sarıçam District, Avcılar Village, plot 18212, parcel 15. The facility is designed to rear 59,400 broilers per production period across a 3,300 square meter two-story poultry house within a 63,131.12 square meter plot, operating 6 periods annually for a total annual production of 356,400 birds. The project site's agricultural land designation, groundwater utilization, flood risks, and proximity to residential dwellings and seasonal streams require rigorous environmental and legal scrutiny.
