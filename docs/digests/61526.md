**Summary (EN):** The proposed project involves the addition of new surface treatment pools (25 m³) to an existing facility located in the Kemalpaşa Organized Industrial Zone in İzmir, bringing the total pool volume to 27,852 m³. The activities comprise metal surface preparation, chemical processing, and static powder coating within existing industrial buildings without any new construction works.
