**Summary (EN):** The proposed basalt quarry capacity increase project by Altyapı Yatırımları Genel Müdürlüğü in Boğazköprü Location, Kocasinan District, Kayseri Province, aims to produce 2,000,000 tons/year of basalt to supply the Yerköy-Kayseri High-Speed Railway Project. The 24.46-hectare EIA area is designated as pasture land on parcel 101/124 and is situated near dry stream beds, agricultural lands, and the Meryemana First-Degree Archaeological Site. The open-pit mining operations involving blasting entail significant environmental risks regarding water resources, agricultural/pasture integrity, archaeological buffer zones, and dust emissions.
