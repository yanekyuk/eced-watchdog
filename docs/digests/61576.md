Ltd. Şti. tarafından yürütülen soğuk hava deposu faaliyetine ilave olarak yıllık 9.600 ton kapasiteli su ürünleri işleme ve paketleme ünitesi kurulması planlanmaktadır. **Summary (EN):** Omega 61 Fisheries Inc. plans to add a seafood processing and packaging unit with an annual capacity of 9,600 tons to its existing cold storage facility located on plot 107, parcel 124 within the Beşikdüzü Organized Industrial Zone in Trabzon, Beşikdüzü. The project involves an investment cost of 8,800,000 TRY, utilizing the existing 1,820 sqm indoor area without any new excavation or construction works. The operations will process trout, seabream, and seabass, with the nearest residential building located approximately 65 meters away from the project site.
