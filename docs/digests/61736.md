**Summary (EN):** MEM Gemi Sanayi ve Ticaret A.Ş. proposes the MEMPORT Port Project covering approximately 47.4 ha across land parcels (Block 140, Parcels 22, 24, 26, and 29) and the adjacent marine area in Denizçalı Mahallesi, Taşköprü, Çiftlikköy, Yalova [s. 19, 49].
