**Summary (EN):** Sayer Gıda İç ve Dış Ticaret A.Ş. proposes to establish a dairy processing facility with a daily capacity of 450,000 liters of raw milk and 650,000 liters of whey on Parcel 36, Block 631 in Umurbey Neighborhood, Ödemiş District, İzmir Province. The land parcel, registered as agricultural field with a title deed area of 47,252.14 m2 and a revised EIA area of 45,677.825 m2, falls within Agricultural Land and Irrigation Area designations under the 1/100,000-scale Environmental Plan. Located approximately 5 km south of Ödemiş town center, the site is 2,290 m from Karakova Neighborhood and 3,600 m from Umurbey Neighborhood. Key environmental considerations include the conversion of designated agricultural and irrigation land, water supply and high-strength wastewater management, and the narrow scope of baseline biodiversity surveys documented only for May 2025.
