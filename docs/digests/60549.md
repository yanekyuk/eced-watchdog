**Summary (EN):** Er İdare Turizm İnş. Gıda Temizlik Hiz. Alımı San. ve Tic. A.Ş. proposes a capacity increase for the Çemişgezek Regulator and Hydroelectric Power Plant (HES) on Tagar Stream in Çukur Neighborhood, Çemişgezek District, Tunceli Province, raising installed capacity from 0.124 MWm / 0.116 MWe to 2.104 MWm / 2.062 MWe [s. 1, 2, 27]. Valued at 141,244,134 TL and classified under Annex-1 Article 14 of the EIA Regulation, the project sits within an area designated as an 'Area of Ecological Importance' (Ekolojik Öneme Sahip Alan) in the 1/100,000 Environmental Plan [s. 2, 27]. A previous 'EIA Positive' decision dated 05.02.2025 was annulled by the Erzincan Administrative Court on 14.01.2026 (Case No. E.2024/293, Decision No. K.2026/81) due to an 840-meter above-ground conveyance canal acting as a barrier for wild goats, a non-functional fishway, and an inadequate one-day flora survey; the current filing is a revised Final EIA Report dated 28.10.2026 prepared under Circular 2009/7 [s. 2, 29]. Primary environmental stakes center on Tagar Stream aquatic habitats, fish populations, otters, wildlife connectivity, 32 hectares of downstream irrigation rights, and unresolved contradictions regarding completed versus planned construction works [s. 13, 14, 27, 29].
