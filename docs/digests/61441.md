**Summary (EN):** The proposed poultry farm capacity increase project by Rıza Zeybek in Manisa Province, Gördes District, Fundacık Village, parcel 156, aims to raise the production capacity from 19,285 broilers/period to 57,600 broilers/period by adding a new 1,919.96 m2 poultry house. The project site is designated as an 'Agricultural Area' and 'Dry Marginal Agricultural Land' in the 1/100,000 scale Environmental Plan, located 85 meters from the nearest residence and 570 meters from Fundacık Village. According to DSİ 2nd Regional Directorate opinions, the site falls within the long-term protection zone of the planned Çağlayan Dam, raising significant environmental concerns regarding water resources, wastewater management, and odor emissions.
